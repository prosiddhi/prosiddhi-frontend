## 1. Executive Summary
**Takeaway:** India's blue-collar hiring, reimagined — a fast, mobile-first, multilingual marketplace that connects frontline workers with employers, monetised on the employer side.

- **The problem** — blue-collar hiring is slow, offline, agency-driven and hard to trust.
- **The product** — a two-sided platform: employers post jobs and search a candidate database; workers find jobs near them, in their own language.
- **The model** — employers pay through flexible credits; **job seekers are free, forever.**
- **The edge** — purpose-built for a **blue-collar, low-literacy, smartphone-first** audience: English + Hindi, smart matching, transparent pay-for-what-you-use pricing.
- **Where it's going** — web platform live today; mobile apps and AI-assisted matching on the near-term roadmap.

## 7. Pricing & Packages
**Takeaway:** Flexible, transparent, pay-for-what-you-use — from a single post to an annual team plan.

- **Two credit types** — **Post credit** (publish a job, live 30 days) · **Download/Unlock credit** (reveal a candidate's full profile once; free to re-view after).
- **Free tier** — search the candidate database (snippets only) at no cost.
- **Free trial** — 1 post + 3 unlocks, valid 14 days, once per employer.
- **All prices are base — 18% GST added at checkout · GST invoice always generated.**
