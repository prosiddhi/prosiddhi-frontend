## 🎨 Designer Notes for Fayaz
- **This is the most visual doc of the set.** The reader may not read much text — **pictures and icons must carry the message.**
- **Bilingual, always:** every headline in **English + Hindi**, stacked. Use a Devanagari-clean font (Noto Sans / Mukta).
- **Big, friendly, few words:** large icons, large tap-target feel, short lines. Think poster/flyer, not corporate deck.
- **The 3-step flow (§3) is the hero** — biggest, clearest element; ideally illustrated (register → search → apply).
- **Warm, aspirational photography:** real, smiling Indian workers who look like the audience (drivers, delivery, helpers, guards) — dignity, not stock.
- **Colour:** friendly and high-contrast for readability on cheap screens in sunlight.
- **Format:** works as a **single-page flyer**, a WhatsApp-shareable image, or a short app-store-style promo — design for mobile-first sharing.
