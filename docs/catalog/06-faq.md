## For Job Seekers

**Is ProSiddhi free for job seekers?**
Yes — it's **completely free, forever**. Job seekers never pay.

**How do I register?**
With your **phone number and an OTP**. You can also use email or Google.

**Do I need Aadhaar?**
**No.** We never ask for or collect Aadhaar.

**How do I find jobs near me?**
Use **Nearby** search, or browse **by category** and **recommended** jobs matched to your profile.

**How do I apply?**
Open a job and tap **Apply** — your profile is sent to the employer. Track it in **My Applications**.

**Can I use the app in Hindi?**
Yes — **English and Hindi** are both available; more Indian languages are coming.

**How will I know if an employer is interested?**
You'll get a **notification**, and you can **chat** with them directly in the app.

## For Employers

**How does pricing work?**
You buy **credits** inside a plan and spend them to **post jobs** and **unlock candidates**. Job seekers are free; only employers pay.

**What are the two credit types?**
**Post credit** — publish a job (live 30 days). **Download credit** — unlock a candidate's full contact (once; re-viewing is free forever).

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes — on activation you get **1 post + 3 unlocks, valid 14 days** (once per employer).

**Why can't I post a job yet?**
**Businesses need admin approval** first (individuals verify by email). This keeps the marketplace trusted.

**Do prices include GST?**
Prices are shown **base + 18% GST** (added at checkout). A **GST invoice PDF** is generated for every purchase.

**Do credits expire?**
Subscription-plan credits expire with the plan (3-day grace). **₹499 pack credits never expire.** Credits are spent soonest-expiring first.

**Can my team share one account?**
Yes — **Pro plans include team seats.** Invite recruiters to one org with a **shared wallet, jobs and unlocks** (owner vs member roles).

**What if I delete a job by mistake?**
Deleting a job returns its post credit **only if** it has **no applications and is deleted within 24 hours** of posting.

## Account

**What are the ways to log in?**
**Phone OTP, email + password, or Google** — for both seekers and employers.

**I forgot my password.**
Use **Forgot password** to reset it via email.

**Can I change my language?**
Yes — **English / Hindi** in Settings.

**Can I delete my account?**
Yes. For job seekers, we stop serving your contact to employers after deletion (per data-protection rules).

## Payments & Refunds

**What payment methods are supported?**
**Razorpay** — card, UPI, and netbanking.

**Are there refunds?**
**No refunds in v1.** Plans run to expiry; to continue, buy again. (Disputes go through Razorpay.)

**Is my payment secure?**
Yes — payments are processed by **Razorpay (PCI-compliant)**; we don't store card data.

**Where do I get my invoice?**
Under **Invoices** — download the **GST invoice PDF** anytime.

## Trust & Safety

**Are employers verified?**
Yes — **businesses are admin-approved** before they can post.

**How are bad job posts handled?**
Posts are **moderated**; seekers can **report** a job, and admins review and act (warn / flag / remove). Automated **content scanning** flags scams.

**Is my data safe?**
We follow **DPDP-aware** data handling, use secure payments, and **never collect Aadhaar**.

**How do you prevent spam/fake jobs?**
Verification + moderation + reporting + AI content scanning together keep the marketplace clean.
