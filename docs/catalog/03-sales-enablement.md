## 1. Elevator Pitch
**Takeaway:** One product, three lengths — pick by how long you've got.

- **10-second:** *"ProSiddhi is India's blue-collar hiring marketplace — hire verified frontline workers faster and cheaper than agencies, and pay only for what you use."*
- **30-second:** add — *"Post a job or search a candidate database, in English or Hindi. Workers join free; you buy credits and spend them only when you post a job or unlock a candidate. No agency mark-ups, no retainers."*
- **2-minute:** add the problem (slow, offline, agency-driven hiring), the two-sided marketplace, the credit model, team seats + GST invoices for businesses, and the roadmap (mobile apps, AI matching).

## 3. ROI Metrics
**Takeaway:** Reframe every price conversation to cost-per-hire vs the agency alternative.

- **Cost per hire** — agencies charge a flat fee or **8–30% of salary**; ProSiddhi is a **fixed, low credit cost** per post/unlock.
- **Time-to-hire** — search-and-unlock a live candidate database vs waiting on agency shortlists.
- **No-show reduction** — verified, moderated candidates + direct chat.
- **No retainers, no per-hire mark-up** — you own the pipeline.

**Worked example (illustrative — [TO CONFIRM real benchmarks]):**
| | Agency | ProSiddhi |
|---|---|---|
| Cost to hire 5 workers | ₹5 × (flat fee ~₹3,000) = ₹15,000 | 1 Starter plan (₹4,499) → 13 posts + 10 unlocks |
| Time-to-first-shortlist | Days | Minutes |
| Own the talent pool after? | No | Yes |

## 4. Pricing Models *(how to sell the plans)*
**Takeaway:** Match the plan to hiring volume — don't over- or under-sell.

- **Single Post (₹499)** — "just try it" / one urgent vacancy.
- **Starter (1/3/6-month)** — small businesses hiring regularly.
- **Pro (6/12-month, 1–3 seats)** — scale, teams, and heavy candidate-database use (200–450 unlocks).
- **Enterprise licensing** — custom volume/terms. **[TO CONFIRM]**
- **Levers:** free 14-day trial (1 post + 3 unlocks) to remove first-purchase risk; GST invoice for business buyers; team seats for multi-recruiter orgs.

## 5. Objection Handling
**Takeaway:** Every common objection has a reframe + a proof point + a question that moves the deal.

| Objection | Reframe / response | Proof | Move-forward question |
|---|---|---|---|
| *"Too expensive."* | Compare to **total cost per hire**, not sticker price — no agency mark-up or retainer. | ₹499 = a hire vs ₹1,000s in agency fees | "What do you pay an agency per hire today?" |
| *"We already use agencies / walk-ins."* | You keep depending on them; here you **own the pipeline** and hire in minutes. | Live candidate database + direct chat | "How long did your last frontline hire take?" |
| *"Do blue-collar workers even use apps?"* | Yes — smartphone-first, **English + Hindi**, icon-led, built for basic phones. | EN/HI complete; mobile-first design | "Do your workers use WhatsApp?" (they do) |
| *"How do I trust candidate quality?"* | Verified employers, moderated posts, taxonomy matching, reviews. | Moderation + reports + verification | "What's gone wrong with quality before?" |
| *"We use [competitor]."* | See battle cards §8 — lead with our differentiator for that rival. | §8 | "What's missing in what you use now?" |
| *"No time to learn a tool."* | Post a job in ~2 minutes; onboarding support included. | 2-min post flow | "Can I show you a 2-minute post right now?" |

## 6. Demo Scripts
**Takeaway:** Show the "wow" moments, not every feature.

**Employer demo (5 min):**
1. Register & land on dashboard.
2. **Post a job in ~2 min** (taxonomy: Category → Sector → Job Title).
3. **Search the candidate database** → show snippet → **unlock one candidate** (the key moment).
4. Schedule an interview + send a calendar invite.
5. Show credit wallet + GST invoice.

**Job-seeker journey (2 min — proves liquidity):**
Register → build profile → search nearby jobs → one-tap apply → chat with employer. *Shows there's real supply on the other side.*

**Wow moments to land:** the 2-minute post · the unlock reveal · the bilingual switch · the shared team wallet.

## 8. Competitive Battle Cards
**Takeaway:** Know where we win, where they win, and the question that sets the trap.

**vs Apna**
- *Their pitch:* large community + networking for blue/grey-collar.
- **Where we win:** employer-grade tools — paid candidate database unlock, team seats, GST invoices, 3-level taxonomy matching.
- *Where they win:* brand awareness, community scale.
- **Trap-setter:** "Can you search and unlock specific candidates, or only wait for applies?"

**vs WorkIndia**
- *Their pitch:* direct employer-worker calls, wide reach.
- **Where we win:** structured taxonomy + candidate database + transparent credit wallet + bilingual, employer team features.
- *Where they win:* established reach in some cities.
- **Trap-setter:** "How do you control per-hire cost as you scale?"

**vs Vahan**
- *Their pitch:* WhatsApp-bot gig hiring, logistics/delivery focus.
- **Where we win:** broader sectors, self-serve employer platform, candidate database, invoicing.
- *Where they win:* deep logistics/gig placements.
- **Trap-setter:** "Do you hire beyond delivery/gig roles?"

**vs Traditional agencies / offline**
- *Their pitch:* done-for-you, relationships.
- **Where we win:** faster, cheaper, you own the pipeline, no mark-up, full transparency.
- *Where they win:* zero effort for the employer.
- **Trap-setter:** "What are you paying per hire — and what happens when you stop paying them?"
