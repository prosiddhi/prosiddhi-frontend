## 🎨 Designer Notes for Fayaz
- **Format:** premium leave-behind — works as a **2–4 page brochure** or a short 6–8 slide deck. More polished than the internal catalog.
- **Audience tone:** business/professional but warm — the buyer is an SME owner or HR/ops manager.
- **Hero + CTA bookends:** strong opening hero (§1) and a clear closing CTA (§7) with contact block.
- **"Why" (§2):** 6-tile benefit grid with icons — the persuasion slide.
- **How it works (§4):** the signature horizontal step-strip.
- **Pricing (§5):** 3 pricing tiles (Try / Small business / Scale) — don't dump the full 8-plan table here; keep it digestible, link to full pricing.
- **Photography:** real Indian frontline workers + a workplace/hiring context. Avoid generic corporate stock.
- **Roadmap items:** keep the *(roadmap)* tags subtle here (small superscript) — this is a sales piece, but don't let it claim unbuilt features as live.
