# Marketing Catalogue — ProSiddhi
### How we talk about ProSiddhi — positioning, messaging & campaign material for the marketing team

> **Framing:** aspirational / target-state. Grounded in ProSiddhi's real identity — a **blue-collar, low-literacy-friendly, English + Hindi** hiring marketplace for India. *(roadmap)* = planned; **[TO CONFIRM]** = needs a real asset.
> **Note on scope:** verticals, SEO and competitors are kept to the **blue-collar** market on purpose — that's the actual product. If you're aspirationally targeting white-collar/GCC segments too, flag it and I'll add a second layer.

---

## 1. Brand Vision
**Takeaway:** India's trusted digital marketplace for blue-collar employment.

- **Vision** — connect millions of frontline workers with trusted employers through technology, transparency and speed.
- **Brand promise** — the right worker, the right job, the right time — in your language.
- **Brand personality** — trustworthy · accessible · local · empowering · no-nonsense.
- **Primary tagline** — *Right Worker. Right Job. Right Time.*
- **Alternates** — *Skilled Workers. Trusted Employers.* · *The Smarter Way to Hire Blue-Collar Talent.*

---

## 2. Mission
**Takeaway:** Connect frontline talent with opportunity — efficiently, and in the worker's own language.

- Empower businesses with reliable workforce solutions.
- Create meaningful, dignified employment for India's skilled and semi-skilled workforce.
- Remove the middleman, the mark-up and the language barrier.

---

## 3. Target Audiences
**Takeaway:** Two sides, spoken to very differently.

| Audience | Who they are | What they want | Message to lead with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Employers (SME/business)** | Shops, factories, warehouses, agencies hiring frontline staff | Fast, reliable, affordable hiring | "Hire verified workers, faster and cheaper than agencies." |
| **Staffing firms** *(roadmap segment)* | Recruiters placing blue-collar at scale | Volume, a talent pool, team tools | "A candidate database and team seats built for volume hiring." |
| **Job seekers** | Blue-collar / semi-skilled workers, often low-literacy, on basic Android | A genuine job near home, in their language, free | "Find real jobs near you — in your language, for free." |

---

## 4. Key Messaging *(pillars: capability → benefit → proof)*
**Takeaway:** Four messages carry the whole brand.

| Pillar | Capability | Benefit | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Faster hiring** | Post & search in minutes | Fill roles before competitors do | Live platform, 30-day job window |
| **Smarter matching** | Taxonomy + AI matching *(roadmap)* | Better-fit candidates, fewer mis-hires | 3-level taxonomy live; AI *(roadmap)* |
| **Jobs near home** | Location + category search | Workers find relevant local jobs | Nearby/category/recommended search |
| **In your language** | English + Hindi *(8 more roadmap)* | Reaches low-literacy workers others can't | EN/HI complete |

## 5. Campaign Themes
**Takeaway:** Local, seasonal, bilingual — meet workers and employers where they are.

- **"Hire Local. Hire Faster."** — the employer speed message.
- **"Kaam, aapke apne sheher mein."** *(Work, in your own city.)* — the worker-resonant Hindi campaign.
- **Festive / peak-season hiring** — Diwali, harvest, delivery peaks (staffing surges).
- **Future Workforce / Digital Recruitment** — thought-leadership for enterprise & staffing buyers.
- **"Empowering India's Workforce."** — brand/PR umbrella.

## 8. SEO Themes
**Takeaway:** Win the searches a blue-collar worker or local employer actually types.

- **Worker intent** — "jobs near me", "delivery jobs in [city]", "driver job", "warehouse job", "helper job", "security guard job", "part-time jobs [city]".
- **Employer intent** — "hire delivery boys", "hire warehouse staff", "blue collar hiring platform India", "staffing for [sector]".
- **Hindi / regional keywords** — naukri near me, [role] ki naukri, etc.
- **Local SEO** — city + role landing pages at scale.

---

## 9. Social Media Themes
**Takeaway:** Educate employers, spotlight workers, build trust — in EN + HI.

- **Worker spotlights** — real placement stories (short video, worker's own words).
- **Employer how-tos** — "post a job in 2 minutes", "how credits work".
- **Sector demand trends** — "top hiring roles this month".
- **Platform explainers** — bilingual, icon-led, short.
- **Channel mix** — YouTube, Instagram Reels, WhatsApp broadcast, regional platforms *(where frontline workers actually are — not LinkedIn-first)*.

## 🎨 Designer Notes for Fayaz
- **Deck type:** brand/marketing deck — warm, human, photo-led (not corporate-cold).
- **Photography:** real, dignified images of Indian frontline workers (drivers, warehouse, delivery, electricians) — avoid stocky white-collar office shots. Get model-released or licensed images.
- **Bilingual by design:** every hero line should have room for a Hindi version beneath the English. Use a font that renders Devanagari cleanly (e.g. Noto Sans / Mukta).
- **Slide 1 = tagline hero** — big "Right Worker. Right Job. Right Time." + Hindi + one worker photo.
- **Messaging pillars (§4)** — render as a **4-tile grid**, each tile = icon + pillar + one-line benefit. This is the money slide; make it the strongest visual.
- **Campaign themes (§5)** — mini mood-board cards, one per campaign, each with its own accent colour.
- **Verticals (§7)** — icon row/grid, one icon per sector.
- **Keep text light on slides** — this deck sells feeling; move detail to speaker notes.
