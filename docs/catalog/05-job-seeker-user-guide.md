# Job Seeker User Guide — ProSiddhi
### Task-based how-to for job seekers · Web (live) + Mobile *(roadmap)*

> **Framing:** describes the real seeker flows. **Audience = low-literacy, first-time smartphone users** — so this guide must be **picture-led, plain-language, bilingual (EN/HI)**. *(mobile roadmap)* where mobile isn't shipped. **[TO CONFIRM]** = verify screen labels against the live app.

---

## 1. Register
**Goal:** Create your free account.

1. Open the app/website → **Register**.
2. Enter your **phone number** → get an **OTP** → enter it.
3. (Or log in with **email** or **Google**.)
4. Done — registration is **free**.

---

## 2. Build Your Profile
**Goal:** Tell employers who you are.

1. Add your **name, location, and work/skills**.
2. Pick your **job categories** (e.g. driver, helper, electrician).
3. Save — a complete profile gets you **more interview calls**.

*Designed to be simple — icon-first, minimal typing.*

---

## 3. Search for Jobs
**Goal:** Find the right job near you.

1. Use **Search** or browse:
   - **Nearby** — jobs close to your location.
   - **By category** — your kind of work.
   - **Recommended** — matched to your profile.
2. Tap a job to see full details.

---

## 4. Apply
**Goal:** Apply to a job in one tap.

1. Open a job → tap **Apply**.
2. That's it — your profile is sent to the employer.
3. Track it under **My Applications**.

---

## 5. Save Jobs & Get Alerts
**Goal:** Never miss a good job.

1. Tap the **bookmark** to save a job for later.
2. Turn on **alerts** to be notified of new matching jobs.

---

## 6. Interviews & Chat
**Goal:** Talk to employers and attend interviews.

1. When an employer responds, you get a **notification**.
2. Open **Chat** to message them directly (text).
3. If you're invited to an **interview**, you'll get the details (and a calendar invite by email).

---

## 7. Notifications
**Goal:** Stay updated.

- You'll be alerted for **interview invites, employer messages, and new matching jobs**.
- **[TO CONFIRM: WhatsApp/SMS alerts — roadmap]**

---

## 8. Change Language
**Goal:** Use the app in your language.

1. Go to **Settings / Language**.
2. Choose **English or Hindi**. *(More Indian languages coming.)*

---

## 9. Your Account
**Goal:** Manage your details.

- Edit your **profile** anytime.
- Manage **saved jobs** and **applications**.
- The app is **always free** for job seekers.
