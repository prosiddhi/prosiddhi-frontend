# Employer User Guide — ProSiddhi
### Task-based how-to for employers · Web (live) + Mobile *(roadmap)*

> **Framing:** describes the real employer flows. Where mobile differs or isn't shipped, it's marked *(mobile roadmap)*. **[TO CONFIRM]** = verify exact screen labels/copy against the live app before publishing.
> **Style:** one task per how-to, numbered steps, plain language.

---

## 1. Getting Started — Register & Get Approved
**Goal:** Create your employer account and unlock posting.

1. Sign up with **phone number (OTP)**, or log in with **email + password** or **Google**.
2. Choose your employer type:
   - **Individual** (e.g. a household) → verify your **email** to activate.
   - **Business** → submit business details/documents → **admin approval** required before you can post.
3. On activation you receive a **free 14-day trial**: **1 job post + 3 candidate unlocks**.

*Note: businesses can't post until approved — this keeps the marketplace trusted.*

---

## 2. Buying Credits / Choosing a Plan
**Goal:** Get the credits you need to post jobs and unlock candidates.

1. Go to **Pricing / Plans**.
2. Pick a plan by hiring volume — Single Post (₹499), Starter, or Pro (teams).
3. Pay via **Razorpay** (card / UPI / netbanking).
4. Credits land in your **wallet**; a **GST invoice** is generated automatically (download anytime).

*Credit types:* **Post credit** (publish a job) · **Download credit** (unlock a candidate's full contact once).

---

## 3. Posting a Job
**Goal:** Publish a vacancy that the right workers can find.

1. Click **Post a Job**.
2. Select the **taxonomy triple**: **Category → Sector → Job Title**.
3. Fill role details, location, and requirements.
4. Publish — this spends **1 post credit**; the job is **live for 30 days**.
5. Editing a published job later is **free**.

*Bulk upload of multiple jobs is (roadmap).*

---

## 4. Managing Applicants
**Goal:** Review and act on people who applied.

1. Open a job → **Applicants**.
2. Review profiles; **accept / reject / shortlist**.
3. Move a candidate to **interview** or **chat** directly.

---

## 5. Searching & Unlocking Candidates
**Goal:** Hire proactively from the candidate database.

1. Go to **Candidate Search**.
2. Search/filter; you'll see **snippets** (contact hidden) for free.
3. To see full profile + contact, click **Unlock** → confirm **"Use 1 credit to unlock?"**.
4. Unlocked candidates stay in **My Unlocked Candidates** — re-viewing is **free forever**.

*You're only ever charged on an explicit unlock confirm — never on an accidental view.*

---

## 6. Interviews & Chat
**Goal:** Talk to candidates and set up interviews.

1. From an applicant/candidate, open **Chat** (text-based) to message directly.
2. **Schedule an interview** → the candidate is notified, and a **calendar (.ics) invite** is sent by email.

---

## 7. Team Seats & Invites *(Pro plans)*
**Goal:** Add recruiters to one shared company workspace.

1. Go to **Team**.
2. **Invite** a teammate by email (within your plan's seat cap).
3. They accept via the invite link → they join your **org**, sharing the **same wallet, jobs and unlocks**.
4. **Owner** can invite/remove and buy plans; **Members** can post, unlock and manage.
5. Remove a member or revoke a pending invite anytime.

*If a plan downgrade drops your seat cap, extra seats auto-suspend (newest first); the owner is always protected.*

---

## 8. Wallet & GST Invoices
**Goal:** Track credits and get your tax documents.

1. **Wallet** shows post/unlock credit balances and expiry.
2. Credits are spent **soonest-expiring first**; pack (₹499) credits **never expire**.
3. **Invoices** → download any **GST invoice PDF** (INV number resets each April).

---

## 9. Account & Settings
**Goal:** Manage your profile and preferences.

1. Update company profile and details.
2. Switch language **English / Hindi**.
